Who Is Autospa

Autospa Hand Wash is an eco-friendly, hand car wash and detailing service based in Portland. Our company was founded back in 2005 by a team of experts with more than 10 years of professional car wash experience. We operate three car washes throughout the Portland area.

Our goal is to provide our customers with the friendliest, most convenient hand car wash experience possible. We use the most modern water reclamation modules and all our products are biodegradable and eco-friendly.

  • Eco-friendly products
  • Trained and skilled crew
